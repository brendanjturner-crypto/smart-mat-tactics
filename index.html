Rules in this prototype

Blue and Red deploy four unique heroes on their nearest row. Blue moves first. On a turn, move one hero to an unoccupied adjacent square (diagonals allowed). All living heroes belonging to that player then damage every enemy in their individual attack tiles, using a simultaneous damage snapshot. Each surviving hero on a landmark earns one point. First to 10 points or to eliminate the opposing team wins. Attacks ignore intervening pieces. The app calculates two equipped passive skills automatically.

This is a balance-test prototype, not a finished product. Passives may be overpowered, there is no AI opponent, and XP persistence, NFC, electronic-card updates and tournament validation are not implemented.
